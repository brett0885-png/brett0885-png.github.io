Support — Tent Hockey & Trick or Treat

Thanks for playing. These are free two-player tabletop games for one iPhone, made by a small independent developer.

How to reach us

The fastest way to report a problem or suggest a fix is to leave a review on the game's App Store page. We read every review, keep a running list of what players report, and ship updates as fixes are ready. That review channel is how these games get better — tell us what you hit and what you'd like to see.

Common questions

How do two people play on one phone? Lay the phone flat between you — each player takes the half facing them. Tent Hockey is air hockey; Trick or Treat is a candy hunt where each side has its own sky.

Does the game need internet? No. Both games work fully offline and collect no data.

Is there a cost? No — the games are free.
