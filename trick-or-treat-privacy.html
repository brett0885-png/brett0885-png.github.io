Trick or Treat — Privacy Policy

Effective: October 25, 2026

Trick or Treat is a two-player Halloween candy-hunt game for iPhone. It is built to be played, not to watch you play it.

Data we collect

None. Trick or Treat collects no personal data of any kind.

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no names or email addresses
  • No analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access
  • No network connections — the game works fully offline
  • Scores exist only in memory while a game is being played and are gone when the app closes

Data we share

None. Because the app collects nothing, there is nothing to share, sell, or send to any third party.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Trick or Treat ever changes how it handles data, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the app's App Store privacy label will be updated to match.

Contact

Questions about this policy: reach us through the Support page for this app, linked from its App Store listing.
